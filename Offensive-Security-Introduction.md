## What I learned:

## How I solved it:

I utilized the dirb command onto the fake bank website to find 2 hidden webpages: an image page and a bank transfer page.
With that information I was able to visit the bank transfer page and use an admin portal to wire money to my account, the way a hacker would.

## Key Takeaways:

The best way to provide a strong defense is by understanding the offense which was the purpose of this introductory exercise.
