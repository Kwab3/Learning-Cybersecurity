## Key Takeaways:

Computers work similarly to the human body with each part playing a separate but important role. 
The Power Supply acts as the heart supplying energy to all other components that require it.
The CPU is the brains which executes instructions when necessary.
The RAM is the short term memory and the SSD/HDD are the long term memory.
The GPU is our visual cortex which translates information into pictures.
The motherboard is the skeletal and nervous system that holds and connects all the components together
The network adapter is our vocal cords that is used to communicate with other systems
And lastly, I/O devices are our senses that are used to obtain information that gets sent to the brain (or the CPU)
